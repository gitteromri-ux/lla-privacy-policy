HEALTH AND WELLNESS INFORMATION WE COLLECT (LONGEVITY LIFE ACADEMY)Added Sept 2026

If you take the Longevity Blueprint course, we collect information about your body, habits and health that you choose to share with us. We collect only what the course actually uses, and only with your explicit opt-in consent.

Definition. “Health and Wellness Information” means any information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies, reveals or could be used to infer your past, present or future physical or mental health status, including bodily functions, vital signs, symptoms, measurements and health-related inferences. It includes everything listed in this section. Health and Wellness Information is treated as “data concerning health” under Article 4(15) and Article 9 of the GDPR and the UK GDPR, as “consumer health data” under United States state consumer-health-data laws, as “sensitive personal information” under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and as “information of special sensitivity” under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law.

Bio-Age Quiz. Before you enrol, you may take our two-minute Bio-Age Quiz. You answer twelve questions about your sleep, energy and daily habits and receive an estimate of how your biology compares with your calendar age. Your answers and the resulting estimate are Health and Wellness Information, because they can reveal or infer information about your health status. If you also leave your name, email address or phone number to receive your result or to speak with an admissions adviser, we link the result to your Contact Form Information.

Admissions Application and Health Intake Questionnaire. Every student completes a short application (background and goals) and a health intake questionnaire before Day One, followed by a personal onboarding review with the Academy team. The questionnaire asks about your current health conditions, medications and supplements, dietary pattern, sleep, movement, stress, relevant medical history and any circumstances the instructor should know about in order to keep the course safe and relevant for you. You may also choose to share existing laboratory results. This information becomes your personal starting point for the eighteen weeks.

Laboratory Results and Biomarkers. The course invites you to work with your own bloodwork: for example ApoB, Lp(a), a cholesterol panel and lipid subfractions, fasting insulin and insulin-sensitivity markers, inflammatory markers, blood sugar, hormones, micronutrients such as vitamin D, and body-composition measurements. You obtain these tests through your own physician or laboratory; we do not order, perform or interpret medical tests. You decide whether to upload results, share them with your instructor, compare them against your previous labs, identify markers you want to move, or re-test a priority marker at day 90. You may redact your name, date of birth, patient identifiers and any results you do not wish to share before you upload a report.

Continuous Glucose Monitor (Abbott Lingo) Information. Every student receives an Abbott Lingo continuous glucose monitor (“CGM”) with access to the Lingo app, shipped to arrive before Lesson 5. To ship it we collect your postal address. The CGM is a consumer wellness biosensor that Abbott provides directly to you under Abbott’s own terms and privacy notice; the glucose readings, food and activity logs and other data that the Lingo app records are collected by Abbott, not by us, and we have no access to your Lingo account. During the course you choose what to bring into class: glucose traces, screenshots, time-in-range and variability figures, your personal glucose-response map (foods that spike you, foods that do not, foods to test), the results of the food-order, meal-timing and post-meal-walk experiments, non-food spikes and your responses to regulation tools. Anything you upload to the learning platform, show on screen, or describe to your instructor or classmates is Health and Wellness Information that we process.

Wearable and Physiological Information. If you own a wearable device, the course helps you triage recovery, sleep and cardio-fitness signals such as heart-rate variability (HRV), sleep data, resting heart rate (seven-day average) and cardio-fitness estimates. We also collect the simple baseline measurements you record yourself during the live baseline assessment: cardio-fitness estimate, grip-strength estimate, sit-to-stand count, and a subjective wellbeing scale. We never connect to your wearable account or pull data from it; you enter or upload the figures you choose to share.

Lifestyle Logs and Self-Assessments. Course assignments include a three-day lifestyle awareness log (sleep, movement, meals, energy), a fasted-morning mood and energy log, a plant-diversity tracker, self-assessments of sleep hygiene (light, temperature, timing, alcohol, caffeine), of your social architecture (close ties, weak ties, community) and of your top stressors, an audit of your current supplement stack and devices, a written prediction of your glucose triggers, and reflective exercises such as visualising your 80-year curve. These logs and assessments are Health and Wellness Information.

Personal Longevity Blueprint. The course ends with your written Personal Longevity Blueprint: your individual plan across nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, supplements and the signals you keep watching, including your CGM synthesis and re-runnable experiments, together with your continuation plan (one habit to defend, one experiment, one number to re-test). The Blueprint is yours. We hold the copy you create on the learning platform so that you can access it, and so that your instructor can review it with you during the course and at the class check-in.

Health and Wellness Information in Class Recordings, Forums and Peer Review. Live sessions are recorded (see “Class Recordings” above). When you discuss your glucose data, laboratory results, symptoms, medications or Blueprint on camera, in the session chat, in the course forum or during peer review, that Health and Wellness Information becomes part of the recording or post and is visible to your classmates and instructor as described in this Policy. You control how much you disclose. You may turn off your camera, use the private chat to your instructor, or share a redacted version of any document.

What we do not collect. We do not collect genetic data, biometric identifiers, precise geolocation, reproductive or sexual-health information, or information about mental-health diagnoses unless you volunteer it in an intake questionnaire or in class, and we do not ask for it. We do not access your medical records, your patient portal, your Lingo account or your wearable account.

Your choice. You are never legally obliged to provide Health and Wellness Information. You can take the whole course while sharing your data only with yourself. If you choose not to share certain information, your instructor will not be able to personalise feedback on it, but you remain entitled to every session, recording, material and the CGM included in your tuition.

ADDITIONAL SAFEGUARDS, RETENTION AND BREACH NOTIFICATION FOR HEALTH AND WELLNESS INFORMATIONAdded Sept 2026

We apply stricter protection, shorter retention and faster breach notification to Health and Wellness Information than to other data.

Explicit opt-in consent. Before we collect Health and Wellness Information we ask for your separate, explicit, opt-in consent through a dedicated checkbox that is not bundled with our Terms of Service, marketing consent or any other consent. The consent request states the categories we collect, the purposes, the parties with whom the information may be shared, and how to withdraw. Sharing Health and Wellness Information with any party other than those listed in this Policy requires a further separate consent. We do not use pre-ticked boxes, dark patterns or general terms to obtain consent, and taking the course is not conditioned on sharing more Health and Wellness Information than the course needs.

Data minimisation. We ask only for information the syllabus uses. You may redact identifiers and unwanted results from laboratory reports, share screenshots rather than full exports, and keep any log private. Instructors are trained not to request information the course does not need.

Access controls and security. Health and Wellness Information is stored in access-controlled systems separated from marketing and sales tools, encrypted in transit and at rest, and available only to staff with a role-based need to see it. Access is logged. We apply the security measures required for databases holding information of special sensitivity under the Israeli Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017, and we have carried out a data protection impact assessment for the Academy’s processing of Health and Wellness Information.

Retention. Bio-Age Quiz answers and results are deleted or de-identified within 12 months of the quiz if you do not enrol. Intake questionnaires, laboratory results, CGM information, wearable figures, lifestyle logs and self-assessments that you have uploaded are retained while you are enrolled and for 24 months after the end of your last Academy course, so that you can complete the class check-in, re-test your priority marker at day 90 and revisit your work; they are then deleted or de-identified unless you ask us in writing to keep them longer, or a legal obligation or legal claim requires retention. Your Personal Longevity Blueprint remains available to you as part of your lifetime access to course materials until you ask us to delete it. Class recordings are retained under the “Class Recordings” provisions of this Policy. You can ask us to delete any Health and Wellness Information at any time under “Your rights” below, and we will also instruct our processors to delete it.

Breach notification. If Health and Wellness Information is acquired, accessed or disclosed without authorisation, we will notify you without undue delay and, where the GDPR applies, notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach where required by Article 33 and notify you under Article 34. Where United States law applies, we will notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 calendar days after discovery, and notify the Federal Trade Commission and, where required, the media, in accordance with the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 CFR Part 318) and applicable state breach-notification laws. Where Israeli law applies, we will notify the Privacy Protection Authority as required by the Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations.

No minors. The Longevity Blueprint course is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect Health and Wellness Information from anyone under 18; if we learn that we have, we delete it.
