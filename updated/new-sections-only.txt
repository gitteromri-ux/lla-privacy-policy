HEALTH AND WELLNESS INFORMATION WE COLLECT (LONGEVITY LIFE ACADEMY)



If you take the Longevity Blueprint course, we collect information about your body, habits and health that you choose to share with us. We collect only what the course actually uses, and only with your explicit opt-in consent.


Definition.
 “
Health and Wellness Information
” means any information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies, reveals or could be used to infer your past, present or future physical or mental health status, including bodily functions, vital signs, symptoms, measurements and health-related inferences. It includes everything listed in this section. Health and Wellness Information is treated as “data concerning health” under Article 4(15) and Article 9 of the GDPR and the UK GDPR, as “consumer health data” under United States state consumer-health-data laws, as “sensitive personal information” under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and as “information of special sensitivity” under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law.


Bio-Age Quiz.
 Before you enrol, you may take our two-minute Bio-Age Quiz. You answer twelve questions about your sleep, energy and daily habits and receive an estimate of how your biology compares with your calendar age. Your answers and the resulting estimate are Health and Wellness Information, because they can reveal or infer information about your health status. If you also leave your name, email address or phone number to receive your result or to speak with an admissions adviser, we link the result to your Contact Form Information.


Admissions Application and Health Intake Questionnaire.
 Every student completes a short application (background and goals) and a health intake questionnaire before Day One, followed by a personal onboarding review with the Academy team. The questionnaire asks about your current health conditions, medications and supplements, dietary pattern, sleep, movement, stress, relevant medical history and any circumstances the instructor should know about in order to keep the course safe and relevant for you. You may also choose to share existing laboratory results. This information becomes your personal starting point for the eighteen weeks.


Laboratory Results and Biomarkers.
 The course invites you to work with your own bloodwork: for example ApoB, Lp(a), a cholesterol panel and lipid subfractions, fasting insulin and insulin-sensitivity markers, inflammatory markers, blood sugar, hormones, micronutrients such as vitamin D, and body-composition measurements. You obtain these tests through your own physician or laboratory; we do not order, perform or interpret medical tests. You decide whether to upload results, share them with your instructor, compare them against your previous labs, identify markers you want to move, or re-test a priority marker at day 90. You may redact your name, date of birth, patient identifiers and any results you do not wish to share before you upload a report.


Continuous Glucose Monitor (Abbott Lingo) Information.
 Every student receives an Abbott Lingo continuous glucose monitor (“
CGM
”) with access to the Lingo app, shipped to arrive before Lesson 5. To ship it we collect your postal address. The CGM is a consumer wellness biosensor that Abbott provides directly to you under Abbott’s own terms and privacy notice; the glucose readings, food and activity logs and other data that the Lingo app records are collected by Abbott, not by us, and we have no access to your Lingo account. During the course you choose what to bring into class: glucose traces, screenshots, time-in-range and variability figures, your personal glucose-response map (foods that spike you, foods that do not, foods to test), the results of the food-order, meal-timing and post-meal-walk experiments, non-food spikes and your responses to regulation tools. Anything you upload to the learning platform, show on screen, or describe to your instructor or classmates is Health and Wellness Information that we process.


Wearable and Physiological Information.
 If you own a wearable device, the course helps you triage recovery, sleep and cardio-fitness signals such as heart-rate variability (HRV), sleep data, resting heart rate (seven-day average) and cardio-fitness estimates. We also collect the simple baseline measurements you record yourself during the live baseline assessment: cardio-fitness estimate, grip-strength estimate, sit-to-stand count, and a subjective wellbeing scale. We never connect to your wearable account or pull data from it; you enter or upload the figures you choose to share.


Lifestyle Logs and Self-Assessments.
 Course assignments include a three-day lifestyle awareness log (sleep, movement, meals, energy), a fasted-morning mood and energy log, a plant-diversity tracker, self-assessments of sleep hygiene (light, temperature, timing, alcohol, caffeine), of your social architecture (close ties, weak ties, community) and of your top stressors, an audit of your current supplement stack and devices, a written prediction of your glucose triggers, and reflective exercises such as visualising your 80-year curve. These logs and assessments are Health and Wellness Information.


Personal Longevity Blueprint.
 The course ends with your written Personal Longevity Blueprint: your individual plan across nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, supplements and the signals you keep watching, including your CGM synthesis and re-runnable experiments, together with your continuation plan (one habit to defend, one experiment, one number to re-test). The Blueprint is yours. We hold the copy you create on the learning platform so that you can access it, and so that your instructor can review it with you during the course and at the class check-in.


Health and Wellness Information in Class Recordings, Forums and Peer Review.
 Live sessions are recorded (see “Class Recordings” above). When you discuss your glucose data, laboratory results, symptoms, medications or Blueprint on camera, in the session chat, in the course forum or during peer review, that Health and Wellness Information becomes part of the recording or post and is visible to your classmates and instructor as described in this Policy. You control how much you disclose. You may turn off your camera, use the private chat to your instructor, or share a redacted version of any document.


What we do not collect.
 We do not collect genetic data, biometric identifiers, precise geolocation, reproductive or sexual-health information, or information about mental-health diagnoses unless you volunteer it in an intake questionnaire or in class, and we do not ask for it. We do not access your medical records, your patient portal, your Lingo account or your wearable account.


Your choice.
 You are never legally obliged to provide Health and Wellness Information. You can take the whole course while sharing your data only with yourself. If you choose not to share certain information, your instructor will not be able to personalise feedback on it, but you remain entitled to every session, recording, material and the CGM included in your tuition.

HOW WE USE HEALTH AND WELLNESS INFORMATION



We use Health and Wellness Information for one purpose only: to teach you the Longevity Blueprint course and help you build your own Personal Longevity Blueprint.


Specifically, we use Health and Wellness Information to: (a) review your admissions application and intake questionnaire so that the instructor can place you in a suitable cohort and adapt the course to your circumstances; (b) ship your CGM to your address; (c) allow your instructor to review your logs, glucose traces, laboratory results and wearable figures with you and give you individual educational feedback during live sessions, office hours and the class check-in; (d) run the course experiments and exercises described in the syllabus; (e) help you draft, finalise and store your Personal Longevity Blueprint; and (f) respond to your questions and support requests about your own data.


Educational, not medical.
 The Academy provides longevity education delivered by certified health coaches, specialists and educators. Our faculty are not your physicians. Nothing in the course, and no feedback on your Health and Wellness Information, is medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for care from your own licensed healthcare provider. Always consult your physician before changing medications, supplements, diet or exercise, and before acting on any glucose, laboratory or wearable reading. We do not use Health and Wellness Information to make decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects on you, and we do not use it for automated profiling or scoring beyond the Bio-Age Quiz estimate you request.


Aggregated course insights.
 We may create statistics that no longer identify you (for example, the share of a cohort that completed the food-order experiment) to improve the syllabus. Such statistics are not Health and Wellness Information and cannot be re-identified.


What we never do with Health and Wellness Information.
 We do not sell it. We do not rent, license or trade it. We do not use it to serve, target, measure or optimise advertising, and we do not transmit it to advertising platforms, data brokers, insurers, employers or lenders. We do not use it to build marketing profiles or to decide which offers you receive. Advertising and analytics cookies on our marketing pages record that a page was visited or a form was submitted; they never receive the content of your Bio-Age Quiz answers, your quiz result, your questionnaire, your laboratory results, your CGM data or any other Health and Wellness Information. We do not use geofencing around healthcare facilities. If you choose to give us a filmed or written review that mentions your health results, that review is governed by the “Feedback” section of this Policy and by your separate, explicit consent to publish it.

WHEN AND HOW WE SHARE HEALTH AND WELLNESS INFORMATION



Health and Wellness Information is shared only with the people and providers needed to run your course, and never for their own purposes.


Your instructor and the Academy team.
 The certified instructor teaching your cohort, and the small Academy admissions and student-success team, see the Health and Wellness Information you submit in order to teach you and support you. Each of them is bound by a written duty of confidentiality.


Your classmates.
 Cohorts are small (eight to fifteen students). Health and Wellness Information you choose to say on camera, type in the session chat, post in the course forum or present during peer review is visible to the other students enrolled in your cohort and to the instructor, and remains visible in the class recording, exactly as described in “Class Recordings” above. Classmates are asked to keep what is shared in class confidential. Information you send privately to your instructor is not shared with classmates.


Abbott (Lingo).
 Abbott provides the Lingo CGM and app to you as an independent controller under Abbott’s own privacy notice. We share with Abbott, or with Abbott’s fulfilment partner, only the information needed to ship your CGM (name, postal address, contact details). We do not receive your Lingo account data from Abbott, and Abbott does not receive your course records from us.


Service providers (processors).
 Health and Wellness Information is stored and processed on our behalf by the providers that host our learning platform, video-conferencing and class recordings, file storage, email and customer-support tools, and by the courier that delivers your CGM (postal address only). These providers act on our documented instructions, are contractually prohibited from using Health and Wellness Information for any other purpose, are bound by confidentiality and security obligations, and must delete or return it when the service ends. The names of the categories of providers, and the specific third parties with whom Health and Wellness Information is shared, are listed in the United States Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy section below.


Our group companies.
 The eTeacher entities named at the top of this Policy may access Health and Wellness Information only to the extent needed to deliver the Academy and to comply with law, under the same safeguards described here.


Legal requirements, claims and corporate change.
 The circumstances set out in “WHEN AND HOW WE SHARE PERSONAL DATA WITH OTHERS” above (legal requirement, violation of law, legal claims, reorganisation of the Service) apply to Health and Wellness Information as well, subject to the additional safeguards in this Policy.


No other sharing.
 We do not share Health and Wellness Information with advertising networks, social-media platforms, analytics vendors, data brokers, insurers, employers or any other third party for their own purposes. We do not share it with any person you have not been told about in this Policy unless you give us separate, explicit consent first.

ADDITIONAL SAFEGUARDS, RETENTION AND BREACH NOTIFICATION FOR HEALTH AND WELLNESS INFORMATION



We apply stricter protection, shorter retention and faster breach notification to Health and Wellness Information than to other data.


Explicit opt-in consent.
 Before we collect Health and Wellness Information we ask for your separate, explicit, opt-in consent through a dedicated checkbox that is not bundled with our Terms of Service, marketing consent or any other consent. The consent request states the categories we collect, the purposes, the parties with whom the information may be shared, and how to withdraw. Sharing Health and Wellness Information with any party other than those listed in this Policy requires a further separate consent. We do not use pre-ticked boxes, dark patterns or general terms to obtain consent, and taking the course is not conditioned on sharing more Health and Wellness Information than the course needs.


Data minimisation.
 We ask only for information the syllabus uses. You may redact identifiers and unwanted results from laboratory reports, share screenshots rather than full exports, and keep any log private. Instructors are trained not to request information the course does not need.


Access controls and security.
 Health and Wellness Information is stored in access-controlled systems separated from marketing and sales tools, encrypted in transit and at rest, and available only to staff with a role-based need to see it. Access is logged. We apply the security measures required for databases holding information of special sensitivity under the Israeli Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017, and we have carried out a data protection impact assessment for the Academy’s processing of Health and Wellness Information.


Retention.
 Bio-Age Quiz answers and results are deleted or de-identified within 12 months of the quiz if you do not enrol. Intake questionnaires, laboratory results, CGM information, wearable figures, lifestyle logs and self-assessments that you have uploaded are retained while you are enrolled and for 24 months after the end of your last Academy course, so that you can complete the class check-in, re-test your priority marker at day 90 and revisit your work; they are then deleted or de-identified unless you ask us in writing to keep them longer, or a legal obligation or legal claim requires retention. Your Personal Longevity Blueprint remains available to you as part of your lifetime access to course materials until you ask us to delete it. Class recordings are retained under the “Class Recordings” provisions of this Policy. You can ask us to delete any Health and Wellness Information at any time under “Your rights” below, and we will also instruct our processors to delete it.


Breach notification.
 If Health and Wellness Information is acquired, accessed or disclosed without authorisation, we will notify you without undue delay and, where the GDPR applies, notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach where required by Article 33 and notify you under Article 34. Where United States law applies, we will notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 calendar days after discovery, and notify the Federal Trade Commission and, where required, the media, in accordance with the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (16 CFR Part 318) and applicable state breach-notification laws. Where Israeli law applies, we will notify the Privacy Protection Authority as required by the Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations.


No minors.
 The Longevity Blueprint course is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect Health and Wellness Information from anyone under 18; if we learn that we have, we delete it.

Legal bases for Health and Wellness Information (Longevity Life Academy).
 Health and Wellness Information is a special category of personal data under Article 9(1) of the GDPR and the UK GDPR. We process it only on the basis of your 
explicit consent
 under Article 9(2)(a), given separately for each specified purpose described in “HOW WE USE HEALTH AND WELLNESS INFORMATION”, together with Article 6(1)(a) (consent) and, for the delivery of the course itself, Article 6(1)(b) (performance of our contract with you). Where we must retain Health and Wellness Information to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, we rely on Article 9(2)(f). You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing 
Privacy@eteachergroup.com
; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal and does not affect your access to the course, its recordings or the CGM. Because the Academy processes health data, we maintain records of processing under Article 30 that identify the Article 6 and Article 9 bases for each purpose, and we have carried out a data protection impact assessment under Article 35(3)(b). Health and Wellness Information is transferred outside the EEA and the United Kingdom only to Israel (recognised by the European Commission as providing adequate protection) and to processors in the United States under the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, with supplementary measures where required.

Additional rights for Health and Wellness Information.
 All of the rights above apply to your Health and Wellness Information. In addition, you may at any time (i) withdraw the explicit consent on which we process it, (ii) ask us to delete all Health and Wellness Information we hold about you, including copies held by our processors, and (iii) receive a copy of your intake questionnaire, uploaded results, logs and Personal Longevity Blueprint in a portable format. We respond to these requests within one month.
